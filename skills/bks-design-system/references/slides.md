# Slides

A slide is read in seconds, from a distance. Tokens come from `DESIGN.md`; this file sets the
rules that the screen guidelines do not cover.

## Grid

- 16:9, 1920 by 1080 px as the design size.
- Outer margin of 96 px; 12-column grid with 24 px gutters; baseline of 8 px.
- One message per slide. Title in the display or headline token, body in the body token scaled up.

## Hierarchy

- Three levels at most per slide: title, key point, support.
- Title at 48 px or larger; body at 28 px or larger; nothing below 24 px.
- Up to six lines of text and about 40 words. More than that is a document, not a slide.
- Number and chart labels are large enough to read without the presenter explaining them.

## Brand on the slide

- The signature appears once: on the cover and on the closing slide, or as a small mark in a
  fixed corner of the content slides. Never both on the same slide.
- Respect the minimum size and the protection area written in `DESIGN.md`.
- Light slide: positive signature. Dark slide: single-color white signature.
- Typefaces and colors only from the `DESIGN.md`; no stock templates with their own look.

## Projector contrast

- Projectors wash out colors: aim for contrast of 7 to 1 on body text, not just AA.
- Prefer the dark theme in dim rooms and the light theme in bright rooms; do not mix on one deck.
- Avoid light blue text on white and thin strokes under 2 px; the brand's light blue is a surface
  or highlight on light slides.
- Never use color alone to carry meaning in a chart; add labels or patterns.

## Checks

- Squint test: the hierarchy is still clear when the slide is blurred.
- Read the deck from the back of the room, or at 25% zoom.
- Export to PDF and check that the fonts are embedded.
